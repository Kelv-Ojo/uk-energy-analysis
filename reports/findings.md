# UK energy: findings and decision context

- Final energy consumption changed by **-12.3%** between 1970 and 2024.
- Consumption rose **2.6%** between 2023 and 2024.
- Transport accounts for **42.2%** of reported final consumption in 2024.

## Suggested next steps

Prioritize transport for deeper demand analysis because it represents a large share of final consumption. Investigate activity, weather and efficiency data before explaining year-to-year changes or setting targets.

## Method and limits

Table C1 is in ktoe. Conversion: 1 ktoe = 0.01163 TWh. Use source totals; never add an aggregate sector to its components. Unavailable fuel cells remain missing rather than becoming zero. Differences in published sector sums are retained as rounding diagnostics. This is a descriptive historical analysis of the local 2025 workbook snapshot, not a current release, causal study, emissions inventory or forecast. Electricity is a delivered fuel and cannot be labelled renewable from this table.

Validation: 55 consecutive years; unique year/sector/fuel keys; nonnegative reported values; complete sector totals. Maximum reconciliation difference: 2.00 ktoe.
